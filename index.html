Quick Guide ??
⚡
Trim / Reset: Give any toe pad a Quick Press to mark it as trimmed. This resets the cycle and starts the countdown. A second quick press undoes it.
⚙️
Tinker Mode: Hold down any toe for 600ms until the White Ring lights up. Rename that particular claw or update its countdown cycle. Tap anywhere outside to close safely.
+
Polydactyl: Use the + and - buttons to add/remove toes (up to 10 per paw).
❌
Amputees: Remove all toes from a paw to reveal the "Remove Paw Slot" button.
☀️
Themes: Switch between Dark Mode and high-contrast Light Mode in the header.
🐾
Care Logs: Tap the clipboard (📋) to see past trims. Wipe your history using Clear Logs, or use Stop Tracking and Start Tracking to pause or resume recording background entries anytime.
📲 App Installation Guide
• Mobile: Tap your browser's Share or Menu button and select "Add to Home Screen" to launch this in full-screen mode with custom local alerts.
• Desktop: Click the App Install icon in your browser's address bar (or the 3-dot menu) and select "Install Page as App" to run it in a clean standalone window.
💾 Profile Backup & Restore
• Export Data: Saves a tiny backup file to your phone or computer. Do this once a month or before clearing your browser history so you never lose your pet charts!
• Import Data: If you get a new phone, clear your history, or tracking timelines go missing, click this button and select your saved backup file to instantly restore your data.
